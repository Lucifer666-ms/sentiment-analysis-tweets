The idea

One sentence. Three possible emotions.

Every message carries a feeling. SENTI.AI reads the words, weighs the signal, and tells you which of three emotions it belongs to — with a measure of how sure it is.

01 / 03Warmth

Positive

Appreciation, delight, satisfaction. Language that lifts — and tells you what people love.

“Honestly the best purchase I've made all year.”Example
02 / 03Friction

Negative

Frustration, disappointment, criticism. The signal that something needs your attention.

03 / 03Balance

Neutral

Facts, questions, observations. Words that inform without leaning either way.

About

Built to read between the lines.

SENTI.AI is a machine-learning sentiment engine trained on real-world language. It pairs word-level and character-level understanding to stay accurate on short, messy, human text — reviews, tweets, support messages — and records every analysis so patterns emerge over time.

3Sentiment classes — positive, negative and neutral. Nothing ambiguous.
2×Feature spaces. Word and character TF-IDF, combined into one signal.
100%Of analyses logged with their confidence, stored securely for review.
